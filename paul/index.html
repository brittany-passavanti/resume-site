Implementation Partner View
HubSpot + Health Score
How I imagine this could scale across multiple CSMs, shared workflows, and AI-supported automation.

1. What I Imagine Exists Today

  • Customer records, account notes, and ownership likely live in HubSpot today.
  • Some onboarding and support activity is probably visible already through existing workflows or Service Hub usage.
  • A lot of account context may still live in CSM habit, memory, and one-off process rather than a shared system.
  • The health picture likely exists in pieces, but not yet in one view that multiple CSMs can consistently act on.

2. What a Stronger Multi-CSM System Needs

  • A shared place for health signals so Brittany, Noah, and an Enterprise CSM are not each working from private context.
  • Clear ownership, consistent onboarding stages, and visible account status across the team.
  • A way for HubSpot to hold what matters most: account properties, task triggers, views, lists, and leadership reporting.
  • A structure where the health score can either live inside HubSpot or be fed into it from product usage, LMS data, and CSM input.

3. What Automation and AI Tools Could Add

  • Trigger alerts when onboarding stalls, usage drops, or admin engagement disappears.
  • Route tasks and follow-up sequences so multiple CSMs are working from the same system, not personal memory.
  • Surface next-best actions, renewal risk, and expansion signals in a way leadership and CS can both act on.
  • Support account summaries, QBR prep, and workflow suggestions so HubSpot becomes not just a record system, but an operating layer.
