Your Thinking & Working Strategy

  • You build systems before you need them. You don't wait for problems to standardize. Your instinct is to build the playbook, the template, the framework — so that scale doesn't mean chaos.
  • You watch signals, not just conversations. You don't wait for clients to tell you something is wrong. You track usage, engagement, and adoption patterns — and you intervene early.
  • You automate what should be automated. Your attention is a finite resource. You design workflows so that the human judgment goes where it actually matters.
  • You think in outcomes, not activities. Every check-in, every onboarding call, every escalation is in service of a measurable result — retention, expansion, time-to-value.
  • You use AI the way Paul would want you to. Claude is in your daily workflow. You built the application site. You're not talking about AI — you're living it.
