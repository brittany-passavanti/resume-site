Services

What I can
help with.

From one room that needs a finishing touch to a whole home getting ready for its next chapter.

(01)

Decorating & room refreshes

We’ll look at what isn’t working and how you want the room to feel, then pull it together with pieces that hold up to everyday life: layout, color, furniture, layered lighting, rugs and textiles, durable fabrics that can handle kids and pets, storage for everyday clutter, and the finishing touches.

(02)

Kids’ rooms & nurseries

A nursery, a big-kid bedroom, a shared room, or a playroom. Fun and full of personality, with durable finishes, toy storage they can actually use, and pieces that can change as your kids do.

(03)

Vacation & second homes

A lake house, a beach place, or a second home you share with family and guests. Welcoming, easy to live in, and ready for sandy feet and muddy paws.

(04)

Staging, occupied or vacant

Living there while it’s listed? I’ll work with your furniture and belongings so each room feels open, calm, and easy for buyers to picture themselves in. Already moved out? We’ll talk through which rooms matter most and what they need to feel finished.

Every home is different, so scope and pricing are quoted individually after I see the space.

(05)

Renovation finish consultations

Building or renovating? I’ll come in and help you choose paint, lighting, flooring, tile, hardware, and fixtures so every finish works together, before anything is ordered or installed.

(06)

Design consultations

Bring your saved photos, your Pinterest boards, AI makeovers of your room, and the room as it is today. Together we’ll sort out what you love, what fits your real space and budget, and what to tackle first.

Already reimagined your room with AI? I’ll help you bring it to life: what’s possible, what it would cost, and where to find the real pieces.

Need more than decorating? I work with trusted partners in Waxhaw on projects big or small, and I manage those relationships for you as much as possible.
